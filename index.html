Who it's for

Three seats at the table

Grimoire serves whoever's playing, not just whoever's GMing.

Your character, at a glance

HP, gear, and light burning down in real time — the state you need for a quick decision, without digging for it mid-scene.

Run the table, not the spreadsheet

Fast bookkeeping for initiative, monster HP, clocks, and NPCs, so the table doesn't stall while you look something up.

Solo or async, still Shadowdark

An AI Game Master with the same real actions a human GM has, and the same visible dice — nothing narrated that wasn't actually rolled.

The idea

Make the pressure legible

Shadowdark runs on a handful of specific pressures — time, darkness, scarce gear, one action per turn, danger that doesn't pull punches. An app for this game only earns its keep if those pressures show up on screen the moment they matter, not as decoration.
