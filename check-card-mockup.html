GM tool system-lines

Not a new component — log_journal_entry's kind: "system" already renders muted regardless of who logged it (LogEntryRow forces ink-faint on system kind, ignoring actor color), so roll_dice and adjust_character_hp's auto-logged lines fall straight into that existing style with zero new CSS. Shown here in context so it's confirmed, not assumed.

Open question, not decided here: should a damage line (HP loss) get a whisper of red and a heal line a whisper of green, the way the stat bar's HP tile already tints red at low HP? Right now every system line is uniformly muted — that's existing shared behavior in LogEntryRow, out of scope for this slice to change unilaterally since it would affect every system line in the app, not just Slice 17's three tools.
